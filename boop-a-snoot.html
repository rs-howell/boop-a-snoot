About

 

Cute critters pop up from their burrows. Tap or click the critter itself (not the burrow) for a boop! You get 30 seconds. They get quicker as the round goes on.

Boop fast for more points — a lightning-quick boop is worth 100, a last-second one is worth 20.

Nobody gets hurt, and missing a snoot costs nothing — it's all boops and hearts.
